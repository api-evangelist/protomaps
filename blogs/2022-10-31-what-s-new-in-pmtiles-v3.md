PMTiles is a single-file archive format for map tiles , optimized for the cloud. Think about it like MBTiles , where the database can live on another computer or static storage like S3; or as a minimal alternative to Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs for any tiled data - remote sensing readings, photographs, or vector GIS features.
